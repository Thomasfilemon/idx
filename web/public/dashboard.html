01 / THE PROJECT

From raw data
to useful insight.

Thomas IDX Lab is a hands-on engineering project exploring how market data can be collected, served, and presented through a lightweight web experience.

The goal isn't to replicate a trading platform. It's to build an understandable, testable foundation — and make market information easier to explore.
